What Prompt Precision Bought in 24 Agent Trials

Observed: All 24 trials in this small synthetic study met the full intended specification on the first pass. Spelling-error mean output stayed within 2% of clear; reduced-grammar runs used 5% more output for Task A and 18% more for Task B; ambiguous-rule runs used 9% more and 27% more. Interpretation: Ambiguous rules transferred rule selection to the agent, so specify governing rules, exceptions, invariants, and success checks first. The one-model, two-task sample, conventional intended answers, non-causal design, and all-success ceiling do not show that ambiguity is safe or that surface form never matters.
