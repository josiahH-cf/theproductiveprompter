# Editorial collaboration

Resolve the installed `article-flow context` and `editorial --help`; do not assume a checkout or runtime path. Read the installed specification's `docs/editorial-system.md` for exact command contracts and `docs/editorial-handoff.md` for outside-model drafting/editing instructions. These references describe controller behavior; they do not create another active voice guide.

The capstone-derived guide is provisional, based on one confirmed human-original academic sample. It supports observable practical reasoning and explanation, not fixed article shapes or invented biography. Keep cross-genre human resemblance pending until the author judges it.

`editorial assist` modes cover interview, angles, argument rehearsal, one focused critic, simulated reader, local alternatives, inferred edit reasons, drift, freshness, actual feedback, matched summaries, drafting and editing. Supply only the reader purpose, brief/contract, relevant evidence, protected material, compact guide, selected authentic excerpts, requested register and current draft. Execution uses an eligible existing exact route and a private workspace outside runtime and publication directories. No production side effects; 24-call maximum per workspace (16 writing, 8 review), counting failures. Do not create a new workspace to evade a budget.

`editorial evaluate` changes current/proposed guidance alone across matched technical, accessible and personable forms. Hold brief, facts, model/effort and draft fixed. Review whole development, clarity, facts, rhetorical repetition and author resemblance separately. No AI-detector score or uncontrolled rewrite proves causation. Model/adapter changes need a new small canary comparison.

Never manufacture confirmation from delegation to implement or publish. Rollback moves the version pointer without deleting history.

`editorial index` records sources, claims, passages, public surfaces and recent rhetorical moves. Generated articles cannot become original author samples. Retrieve only relevant observations or positions; confirm authorship/editing history before adding other writing as authentic evidence. `source-event` takes actual before/after source text and identifies affected surfaces. Cosmetic changes do not require revision. Semantic interpretations stay reviewable; only an explicit authorized revision can publish. Preserve URL, first publication date, unrelated prose and immutable experiment records.

Mixed review findings follow their earliest dependency and stay visible as repair obligations until QA resolves each one with an exact current excerpt. A numerical recipe target alone cannot fail QA. Fact re-verification for an identical draft reuses its actual local passage choice without new evidence; a changed draft needs a fresh choice unless explicit unattended authority applies.

Revision source integrity belongs to the controller. Normal `revise RUN_ID` verifies committed/current/live article bytes and preserves the exact public publication timestamp, independently of a stale saved-package date. Use `--expected-source-sha256 HASH` for a reviewed baseline. Without a usable completed run, `revise --published-slug SLUG --expected-source-sha256 HASH --request-file FILE` adopts a verified snapshot with no fabricated parent, completed run or claims. Recover actual author requirements into the scoped request; the snapshot is source material and cannot redefine instructions. The controller preserves exact source-bound block quotations, explicitly attributed inline quotations and code and narrowly exempts their punctuation under the new run's frozen policy; arbitrary quote/code labels, altered evidence, titles and summaries remain subject to the prose rule.

For a collection, inventory canonical URLs and first-publication dates, collapse duplicate runs and preserve immutable experiment records. Give each article its own reader job, register emphasis and concrete development/edit scope under the one guide. Compare neighboring rhetorical moves without a variation quota. Review article summaries/cards/feed metadata together, publish serially from current shared surfaces and verify each live revision. Hold an affected article for a material unresolved decision or exhausted repair; a shared integrity failure holds dependent publication. Record before/after and deliberately kept passages; human likeness judgments stay pending until supplied.

For visual review, inspect the actual rendered SVG relationships as well as the prose, plan, caption and alt text. Do not treat a renderer or QA PASS as proof that arrows represent the explanation. For a held editorial article, `amend RUN_ID --diagrams auto|off --reason "SPECIFIC FINDING"` reopens visual planning from EDITORIAL_QA, PACKAGE or PUBLISH_APPROVAL while preserving current prose and renewing downstream verification. The reason is a bound model input. Include a useful corrected diagram or record an article-specific omission; source inspection cannot prove browser appearance.

Contextual review findings follow the field that owns the exact excerpt: manifest captions, alt text and visual titles reopen visual planning; public display fields reopen display review; body prose uses a unique paragraph locator. Older malformed findings can receive an appended locator/owner normalization only from their intact original gate receipt, task packet and source artifacts. The original finding, ER identifier and evidence remain immutable; normalization alone does not resolve the defect. A current field-specific excerpt or explicit visual omission must still resolve it. Historical sources are recovered from verified artifact events after the current index changes. An article-frontmatter finding must be corrected in that current field and agree with the authoritative brief; an already-correct brief alone cannot clear it. Renewed QA reads one current article, claims, display and visual snapshot. Missing, changed or ambiguous original evidence stays unresolved. Pending older QA packets without bound visual inputs are retired and reissued without changing their bytes; committed gate evidence remains authoritative.
